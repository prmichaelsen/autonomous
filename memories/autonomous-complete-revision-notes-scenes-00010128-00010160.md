> Complete revision notes for Dex's investigation and confrontation scenes.

## Story Logic Fix

**Original Issue:** Manifests showing "Middle East" destinations was unrealistic for domestic military/surveillance deployment.

**Solution:** Vehicles are shipped to cover destinations that sound legitimate but are actually:
1. Military-adjacent facilities in Nevada/Arizona (near bases)
2. Municipal "partnerships" for surveillance deployment
3. Shell companies registered in Cayman Islands with no traceable presence

## Scene 0001_0128 (Discovery - Night at Aivia HQ)

**On-Screen Manifest Shows:**
- Fleet Testing Facility - Nevada
- DOT Research Center - Yuma, AZ
- Desert Testing Facility, NV
- Municipal Fleet Services - Various Cities
- Urban Deployment Study (Purpose)
- Public Safety Initiative (Purpose)
- Vehicles marked "decommissioned" or "recalled for updates"
- Shell Companies: Apex Logistics Solutions, Meridian Transport Group, Continental Fleet Services, TRG Holdings LLC, Consolidated Services Group, Vector Systems Integration, Strategic Resources LLC

**Dex's Reactions (talking to himself):**
1. "Fleet Testing Facility... Nevada... we don't test in Nevada..."
2. "DOT... Municipal Fleet Services in cities all around the country... what does that mean?"
3. "Apex Logistics... TRG holdings... why are we shipping vehicles direct to all of these companies..."

## Scene 0001_0160 (Confrontation - Clayton's Office, Next Day)

**Dex's Exact Dialogue:**
"Have a look at this. We have entire fleets worth of vehicles with no location data—or the location data is restricted—but if you look at the shipping manifests, we can see them routed all over the country to generic 'testing facilities' and local municipalities. Then some of these vehicles were routed to companies like 'TRG Holdings.' I looked up TRG Holdings—it has no presence online and it's registered in the Cayman Islands. I looked up some of these other companies... all registered in the Cayman Islands, no websites, nothing online."

This evidence presentation leads directly to Clayton calling in the FBI and Dex's arrest.
