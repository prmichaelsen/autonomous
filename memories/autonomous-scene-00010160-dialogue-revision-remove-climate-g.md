> Scene 0001_0160 - Dialogue Revision

## Changes to Make:

**REMOVE:**
- All references to "climate initiative grants" and funding discrepancies
- Dex discovering financial records
- Clayton will discover the funding issues on his own later

## New Dialogue:

_DEX presents his findings about missing vehicles with restricted location data and suspicious shipping manifests._

**Clayton**: Nothing is hidden from me.

**Dex**: So you do know where these vehicles are going? And why?

**Clayton**: Do you think I have time to worry about shipping manifests?

> ALT: "I pay other people to look at shipping manifests, Dex. I pay you to build vehicles that don't _murder_ people."

**Dex**: Clayton, if our vehicles are being shipped off to undisclosed locations to third parties, how can we be sure what is being done with them? What if they're being tampered with? This could explain how something went wrong in our systems — if we have no control of the systems —

**Clayton** _(barks)_: DEX! I gave you 24 hours to find the source of this crash and you've given me nothing!

_Pregnant pause._

**Clayton**: Someone is leaving here in handcuffs today, and it's not me, Dex.

_The flash of red and blue lights begin to illuminate the outside window._

**Clayton**: Time's up, Dex.

## Story Logic:
- Keeps focus on the shipping manifest mystery
- Removes Dex's financial investigation (Clayton discovers that himself later)
- Clayton's responses are more evasive and defensive
- Dex's theory: tampering by third parties could explain the crash
- More tension, shorter exchange
- Clayton's threat is more direct and chilling
