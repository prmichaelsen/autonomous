> AUTONOMOUS - Ravel Motivation & Dialogue (User's Original Ideas)

AUTONOMOUS - Ravel Character Motivation (User's Brainstorm)

## User's Original Brainstorming on Ravel's Motivation

### Core Questions:
- Why does Ravel let Dex go?
- What can Dex do that Ravel can't?
- Why has Ravel kept quiet on the subject?

### Ravel's Backstory (User's Concept):
When Ravel tried to investigate Emma's death, his wife and extended family all began receiving death threats. Maybe other nefarious things started happening, so he fell in line.

### The Breaking Point:
Dex doesn't have a choice. By cutting Dex loose, Dex has one shot to expose the whole thing.

### Why Ravel Risks His Neck:
Because Ravel hasn't been living every single day since his daughter was taken. Whatever they do to him can't be worse than what they've already done to him. His wife left him for not fighting back. He retreated into work, selling his time to the very people that forced him to stay silent about what happened. This is his chance to finally turn the tide, level the playing field.

### The Key Question: "Why not do it himself? Why wait until Dex is pinched?"

## User's Refinement:
Actually, Ravel was given orders to dispose of Dex, but he decides not to. This creates the dynamic where Ravel has to PRETEND to hunt Dex while actually trying to help him by turning over the information he has.
