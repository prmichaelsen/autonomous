> AUTONOMOUS - Ravel/Dex Dialogue: "I've Done My Research"

AUTONOMOUS - Ravel/Dex Scene Dialogue Fragment

## Key Exchange - Ravel Reveals His Research

```
RAVEL: I've done my research on you. No 
       family, no spouse.

DEX: You've done your research on me?

RAVEL: I've researched everyone who works 
       at Aivia, especially engineering. 
       It's the one thing that has kept me 
       hanging on. Hanging onto some way... 
       I can't bring her back... but...
```

## What This Reveals:

### Ravel's Obsession:
- He's been researching Aivia engineers for YEARS
- Not as part of his job - as personal vendetta/hope
- Looking for someone who could help expose the truth
- This is what's kept him alive - the possibility of justice

### Why Dex Specifically:
- No family = no leverage for threats
- No spouse = nothing to lose
- Engineering expertise = can prove the backdoor
- Already targeted = already burned, motivated

### The Tragedy:
- "It's the one thing that has kept me hanging on"
- Ravel's entire existence since Emma's death has been this research
- Waiting for the right person, the right moment
- Dex is that moment

### Character Depth:
- Ravel isn't just passively broken
- He's been ACTIVELY preparing for this
- Years of research, waiting, planning
- Dex falling into his lap is fate/opportunity

## Scene Context:
This dialogue belongs in Scene 0002_0008 when Ravel is supposed to dispose of Dex but instead lets him go. The research revelation explains WHY Ravel has been waiting and WHY Dex is the perfect proxy.
