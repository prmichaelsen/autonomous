> AUTONOMOUS - Plot Clarification: Dex Doesn't Know About Backdoor Yet

AUTONOMOUS - Critical Plot Clarification: Who Knows What When

## User's Important Correction:

"Dex doesn't know about the backdoor system yet, only Nova does"

## Knowledge Distribution at Start:

### Dex Knows:
- He works at Aivia (engineer)
- He's being framed for something
- He's in FBI custody
- He's about to be disposed of
- **He does NOT know about the backdoor yet**

### Nova Knows:
- The backdoor exists
- Aivia vehicles are being used for assassinations
- The pattern of "accidents"
- **She has discovered/uncovered this independently**

### Ravel Knows:
- His daughter Emma was killed by an Aivia Ghost
- It wasn't an accident (accelerated through intersection)
- There's a conspiracy/cover-up
- He was threatened into silence
- **He suspects but may not have technical proof of backdoor**

## Story Implications:

### Dex's Arc Must Include:
- Discovery of the backdoor (not knowing about it from the start)
- Learning what he's really been framed for
- Understanding the scope of the conspiracy
- This is a REVELATION for him, not something he already knows

### Nova's Role:
- She's ahead of Dex in understanding the conspiracy
- She needs Dex for his insider access to Aivia systems
- She has to TELL/SHOW him about the backdoor
- Their meeting is when Dex learns the truth

### Ravel's Plan:
- He can't tell Dex about the backdoor (he doesn't have technical proof)
- He knows something is wrong, suspects weaponization
- He's sending Dex out hoping he'll discover what Ravel suspects
- OR he knows Nova knows and is counting on her to enlighten Dex

## Critical Scene Order:
1. Ravel lets Dex go (Dex still doesn't know about backdoor)
2. Dex is on the run, confused about what he's really accused of
3. Nova finds Dex (or Dex finds Nova)
4. Nova reveals the backdoor to Dex
5. Dex realizes the scope of what he's caught up in

This changes the "what can Dex do" question - Ravel is gambling that Dex will DISCOVER the truth and then be able to prove it from the inside.
