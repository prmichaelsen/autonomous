## AUTONOMOUS Film: Signal Jammer Technology - GROUNDED IN REALITY ✅

### Technical Verification: Wi-Fi/RF Jammers CAN Disable Robot Dogs

**Real-World Evidence:**

1. **Boston Dynamics Spot Vulnerability**
   - Operates in manual mode via Wi-Fi controller
   - Wi-Fi jammer would immediately disable remote operation
   - Source: The Independent confirmed "the Digidog can also be disabled with a Wi-Fi jammer"

2. **DHS NEO Robot Dog with Jammer (Reverse Scenario)**
   - Department of Homeland Security uses "NEO" (modified Ghost Robotics Q-UGV)
   - Carries onboard computer and antenna array to create denial-of-service (DoS) attacks
   - Purpose: Disable IoT devices during police raids
   - This proves the technology works - they use robot dogs WITH jammers, but same tech can jam the dogs themselves
   - Source: Schneier on Security blog (July 2024)

3. **Boston Dynamics Communication Methods**
   - Wireless communications for remote operation
   - Wi-Fi control for manual operation
   - Cellular/radio frequencies for long-distance control
   - If they lose signal, they retrace steps to last known connection point
   - Source: BBC News

**Technical Considerations:**

✅ **What Makes It Realistic:**
- Signal frequency jammers are real tech (used in bomb disposal, VIP protection since 1980s)
- Robot dogs rely on wireless communication to operate remotely
- Off Grid Man would realistically have this - defensive tech against surveillance
- Creates a "dead zone" where remote-controlled devices stop working

⚠️ **Limitations to Consider:**
- Range limitations - Jammers have limited effective range (power decreases by square of distance)
- Bandwidth - Need broadband jammer to cover multiple frequencies (Wi-Fi, cellular, radio)
- Legal gray area - FCC prohibits jammers in US, BUT exceptions exist for government/law enforcement (Off Grid Man doesn't care about laws anyway)
- Not a magic bullet - Dogs with autonomous operation (AI-driven, no remote control) would still work

**Screenplay Application:**

Perfect for AUTONOMOUS because:
1. Grounded in reality - audiences can verify this is real technology
2. Shows Off Grid Man's preparedness against surveillance threats
3. Creates dramatic tension - pursuing dogs suddenly stop at invisible perimeter
4. Plausible for character - someone truly off-grid would have anti-surveillance countermeasures
5. Not too sci-fi - current technology, not future tech

**Suggested Scene Elements:**
- Dex approaches and notices dogs suddenly freeze at invisible perimeter
- Off Grid Man dialogue: "Broadband jammer. Nothing with a wireless signal gets past that fence. Not even your Boston Dynamics friends."

**VERDICT: Realistic movie tech, NOT movie fudging!** One of the most technically accurate elements in the script.
