AUTONOMOUS - CLARIFICATION: Nova's Background as Penetration Tester

Nova wasn't working FOR Dex and Clayton at Aivia. She was hired as an independent **penetration tester** to audit Aivia's security systems.

## The Setup:

- Nova is a professional penetration tester (ethical hacker)
- Aivia hired her to test their security infrastructure
- During the penetration test, she discovered the backdoor in the autonomous vehicle code
- This was NOT what she was hired to find—it's way bigger than a security vulnerability
- She realizes this is evidence of a conspiracy to commit assassinations

## The Social Engineering Scene Recontextualized:

When Nova social engineers Clayton's assistant to get access to his location/biometrics, she's using her **professional penetration testing skills** for an unauthorized purpose. 

The dialogue still works:
- "his smart watch! it transmits mr stanford's biometrics so i can monitor his vitals"
- Nova tricks the assistant into revealing this information OR giving her access to the tracking system
- The assistant thinks Nova is legitimate (maybe IT support, security audit, etc.)
- "can also have the main characters social engineer an assistant to get access to his location"

## Key Points:

1. **Nova's legitimate access**: As a pen tester, she had authorized access to Aivia's systems initially
2. **The discovery**: She found the backdoor during her security audit
3. **Going rogue**: After discovering the conspiracy, she goes beyond her authorized scope
4. **Her skillset**: Social engineering, hacking, penetration testing—all professional skills
5. **Not an insider**: She's an external contractor, which means:
   - She has no loyalty to Aivia
   - She can walk away (theoretically)
   - She's not complicit in the cover-ups
   - She has plausible deniability initially

## Story Implications:

- Nova was doing her job when she stumbled onto something massive
- She wasn't looking for a conspiracy—she was testing security
- This makes her decision to act more meaningful: she could have just finished her contract and left
- Her pen testing background explains how she's so good at infiltration and social engineering
- It also explains why she knows how to cover her tracks

This is a much cleaner origin for Nova and explains her capabilities without making her an Aivia employee.
