> AUTONOMOUS - Complete Synopsis

# AUTONOMOUS - COMPLETE SYNOPSIS

## LOGLINE
When a lead engineer at a self-driving car company is framed for a fatal accident, he uncovers a conspiracy to use autonomous vehicles as remote-controlled assassination weapons—and becomes the next target.

---

## ACT 1: THE ACCIDENT & THE FRAME-UP

### Opening: The Beggar's Warning
Outside Aivia headquarters, a disheveled man tries to warn CEO **Clayton Stanford** about the company's vehicles. Before he can deliver his message, an **Aivia Ghost** (autonomous vehicle) runs a red light and kills him. His dying words: "Stop... Aivia..."

### The Cover-Up Begins
**Dexton "Dex" Sharp**, Aivia's lead engineer, investigates the accident. The system shows 99.9% precision—yet the vehicle accelerated *toward* the victim. Clayton pressures Dex to find a scapegoat within 24 hours or face consequences.

### The Pattern Emerges
Dex discovers anomalies: missing vehicles flagged as "decommissioned" but still transmitting signals. Someone has been using Aivia's fleet for something other than transportation.

### Clayton's Discovery
Clayton investigates Aivia's funding and discovers **military connections**—massive payments from defense contractors. He confronts his handler, **Varick**, who coldly reveals the truth: the "accidents" were **targeted assassinations**. Aivia's vehicles are being weaponized.

### Dex Gets Framed
A child is killed by an Aivia Ghost—and Dex is arrested as the scapegoat. The system logs are altered to implicate him. He's taken into custody by **Agent Ravel**, an FBI agent whose own daughter, **Emma**, was killed by an Aivia "accident" three years ago.

---

## ACT 2: THE CONSPIRACY DEEPENS

### Ravel's Choice
Ravel was **ordered to dispose of Dex** (make it look like suicide). Instead, he sees an opportunity: Dex has the technical knowledge, the access, and nothing left to lose. Ravel takes Dex to a remote location and reveals everything:
- Emma's death was no accident—the vehicle *aimed* for her
- Ravel has been researching Aivia engineers for years, waiting for someone who could fight back
- "You're Clayton's only friend. You can get close to him."

Ravel releases Dex with a mission: **expose the truth**.

### Dmitry's Betrayal
Dex goes to his coworker **Dmitry** for help—unaware that Dmitry is the **primary kill bot operator** under Varick's command. Dmitry sets up a fake "reporter meeting" and sends Dex into an assassination trap.

### The Kill Bot Hunt
An Aivia Ghost (red glow = kill mode) hunts Dex through the city. The vehicle slams into him, injuring his leg. Just as it's about to finish him, **green lights** flood the scene—the kill bot crashes.

**Nova**, a woman with tech that disables kill bots, rescues him. "You're even stupider than I imagined."

### The Safe House
Nova takes Dex off-grid and explains everything:
- There's a **backdoor** in Aivia's system that allows remote control
- She has a program that can shut down the entire kill bot network
- But it requires physical access to Varick's **stronghold** (fortified base)
- **Dex needs to bait Clayton into helping**

Dex realizes: "You're planning to kill Clayton."

Nova doesn't deny it. "How many people have died because of him?"

### The Phone Call
Dex calls Clayton. Clayton—who just learned about the assassinations from Varick—is suspicious and **declines to meet**. "I can't help you, Dex."

### Clayton's Second Confrontation
Clayton returns to Varick, demands the full truth. Learns the extent: dozens of deaths, Emma Ravel, the cover-ups. He's horrified. Realizes: **Dex was right**.

Clayton calls Dex back: "You were right. I'll meet you. Tell me where."

### Clayton's Death
Before they can meet, **Varick has Clayton killed** (vehicle crash). Nova was already nearby—when Clayton declined the first call, she knew Plan B was necessary: kill Clayton, take his phone for access credentials.

Dex sees the crash. His only friend was coming to help him—and died trying.

### Nova's Death
Back at the safe house, Dex and Nova prepare for the assault. Suddenly, the **Mammoth** (massive Aivia assault vehicle) crashes through the building. **Nova is pinned against the wall by the Mammoth**. The vehicle crushes her.

Dex barely escapes. Nova dies before his eyes.

He's alone now. But he has everything he needs: Clayton's phone, Nova's program, and nothing left to lose.

---

## ACT 3: THE ASSAULT & THE SHUTDOWN

### The Evidence Goes Public
Using Clayton's phone, Dex uploads the evidence:
- Footage of Emma Ravel's death
- Other assassinations
- Military funding documents
- Internal communications

**Mass unrest**: Protests, riots, demands for Aivia's shutdown.

### The Plan
Dex executes Nova's strategy alone:
1. **Create false cell phone pings** to make Varick think he's in the city
2. Varick mobilizes his forces (including the **Gatling Ghost**—heavily armed kill bot)
3. **Stronghold is left vulnerable** (only 2-3 Ghosts + Dmitry guarding)
4. Dex infiltrates, uploads Nova's program to the central server
5. Use the backdoor to stop Varick when he returns
6. Trigger **mass self-destruct** of all Aivia vehicles

### The Infiltration
Dex sneaks into the stronghold, reaches the server room. Begins upload: **3-minute timer**.

Progress bar: 5%... 10%... 25%...

### Dmitry's Last Stand
**Dmitry appears.** "You really thought we'd leave this place unguarded?"

They fight. Brutal, desperate. Dex is already injured (leg). Dmitry is trained, efficient.

Progress bar: 55%... 75%...

The fight spills onto a **balcony**. They grapple near the edge. The railing **breaks**. Both fall.

**Dmitry lands first** (dies instantly). **Dex lands on top** (Dmitry's body breaks his fall). Dex survives—barely. Leg worse, bleeding, possibly broken ribs.

Progress bar: 98%... 99%...

Dex drags himself back to the server room.

Progress bar: **100% - COMPLETE**.

### Varick's Return
Varick realizes the trap. Races back in the **Gatling Ghost**. Finds Dex collapsed on the ground, broken and bleeding.

**Varick toys with him:**
- Gatling gun fires rounds *around* Dex (deliberate misses)
- Psychological torture: "Clayton begged. He called your name."
- "That woman at the safe house—she died screaming."
- Aims for the killshot (red targeting laser on Dex's forehead)

"Any last words?"

**Dex stalls** (watching the progress bar on the terminal):
- 96%... 97%... 98%...
- Asks about Emma Ravel (buying time)
- Varick: "I don't keep track of names."
- 99%... **100% - SYSTEM OVERRIDE ACTIVE**

Dex smiles through blood: "You should have."

### Varick's Death
The Gatling Ghost **lurches**. Varick: "What's happening?!"

Vehicle **reverses at full speed**. "I'VE LOST CONTROL!"

**SLAMS into a concrete wall.** Explosion. Fireball. Varick dies screaming.

Dex watches the flames: "That's for Clayton. And Emma. And Nova. And everyone."

### The Final Command
Dex enters the stronghold control room. Runs Nova's program:

**EXECUTE: GLOBAL_SHUTDOWN**  
**CONFIRM: SELF_DESTRUCT_ALL_UNITS**

### The Self-Destruct
**Montage across the world:**
- Aivia Ghosts suddenly stop
- Red glow intensifies
- **Explosions**
- One by one, every Aivia vehicle self-destructs
- Flames, wreckage
- **The killing stops**

Citizens watch: shock, relief, grief, uncertainty.

News coverage: "All Aivia vehicles have been disabled. The autonomous vehicle network has collapsed."

### The Aftermath
Dex walks out of the stronghold, surrounded by burning vehicles. Smoke rising. Sirens in the distance.

He limps away from the wreckage. Alone. Everyone who helped him is dead.

But the fight is over. For now.

## KEY CHARACTERS

**Dexton "Dex" Sharp**: Lead engineer, framed and hunted, becomes whistleblower  
**Clayton Stanford**: CEO, discovers the truth too late, killed trying to help  
**Agent Ravel**: FBI agent whose daughter was killed, releases Dex as proxy fighter  
**Varick**: Military handler, architect of the assassination network  
**Dmitry**: Dex's coworker, secretly the primary kill bot operator  
**Nova**: Mysterious hacker with kill bot countermeasures, saves Dex, killed by the Mammoth at the safe house  
**Emma Ravel**: Agent Ravel's 6-year-old daughter, killed by Aivia Ghost  
**Mina Canario**: First engineer to discover the backdoor, killed before she could expose it

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

**Red Glow**: Aivia vehicles in "kill mode"  
**Green Lights**: Nova's countermeasure (disables kill bots)  
**Progress Bar**: Upload timer during climax (creates unbearable tension)  
**The Gatling Ghost**: Heavily armed kill bot (Varick's weapon)  
**The Mammoth**: Massive assault vehicle (kills Nova at the safe house)  
**Explosions**: Global self-destruct sequence (all vehicles destroyed)

---

## THE ENDING

The autonomous vehicle network is destroyed. The conspiracy is exposed. But the victory is pyrrhic:

- Dex lost his only friend (Clayton)
- Nova sacrificed herself (killed by the Mammoth at the safe house)
- Dex completes the mission entirely alone
- Thousands lost their transportation (civilian vehicles destroyed too)
- The technology is gone, but the people who enabled it remain
- Someone will build something else

**Dex's final realization**: Technology isn't the enemy. The people who weaponize it are. And they'll try again.

The fight isn't over. It's just beginning.
