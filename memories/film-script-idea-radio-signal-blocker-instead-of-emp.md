> Film Script Idea: Radio Signal Blocker Instead of EMP

AUTONOMOUS film script idea: Replace EMP with radio signal frequency blocker/jammer.

KEY SCENE: As Dex approaches the home of Off Grid Man, Off Grid Man has a signal frequency jammer that stops the Boston Dynamics dogs from being able to operate remotely.

TACTICAL ELEMENTS:
- Radio signal frequency blocker can stop Boston Dynamics dogs
- Can also stop the kill bots
- Stop sign can still be used to connect the cable bots (they're connected to the signal)
- Dex can use the dog camera he finds to tell Varrick that he's coming to the chirp HQ to reveal everything
- This will mislead American and Sever, who will send their army of people to that fake location
- This allows Dex to have less resistance at the Varrick headquarters (forces are split)

NARRATIVE ADVANTAGE: More realistic than EMP, creates tactical deception opportunity, uses enemy surveillance tech against them.
