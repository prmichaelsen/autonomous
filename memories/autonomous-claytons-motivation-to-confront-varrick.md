> AUTONOMOUS - Clayton's Motivation to Confront Varrick

AUTONOMOUS - Clayton's Motivation to Confront Varrick (Scene 0001_0160 Aftermath)

## Clayton's Character Arc Shift

**Previous Motivation:** Unclear why Clayton confronts Varrick

**NEW MOTIVATION:** 

After Dex's arrest, Clayton realizes the missing vehicles are REAL. He's furious because:

1. **It's above his head** - Something is happening that Clayton doesn't control
2. **He's tied up in something shady with Varrick** - He knows there's corruption but never knew the details
3. **Not knowing pisses Clayton off** - He knows everything. He's in control of everything. Always.
4. **It affects HIS bottom line** - Almost 100 missing vehicles that he only found out about through Dex
5. **He's been kept in the dark** - This is personal now

## New Scene Addition (After Dex's Arrest)

**INT. CLAYTON'S OFFICE - MOMENTS AFTER DEX IS LED OUT**

Clayton stands alone in his office, staring at the chaos outside. He picks up his phone.

**CLAYTON**
"Hi Varrick, yes, Clayton. Are you free? I think it's a good time we sync on this Dexton Sharp issue. Yes. Yes. I'll be there."

He hangs up. His eyes drift to the tablet Dex left on the table—the one with all the shipping manifest data. 

His eyes narrow.

He swipes it up and walks briskly out the door.

## Character Insight:

Clayton's control is his identity. Learning that vehicles are disappearing above his paygrade is a direct threat to his ego and power. He framed Dex to protect himself, but now he realizes he might be a pawn too. That infuriates him more than anything.
