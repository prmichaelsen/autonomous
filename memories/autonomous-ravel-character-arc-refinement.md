> AUTONOMOUS - Ravel Character Arc Refinement

AUTONOMOUS - Ravel Character Motivation Refinement

## New Understanding of Ravel's Arc

### The Setup:
- Ravel is ORDERED to dispose of Dex (kill him quietly, make it look like suicide/accident)
- Instead, he decides to set Dex loose as a proxy fighter
- Ravel then has to PRETEND to hunt Dex while actually trying to help him

### Why This Works Better:
1. **Higher stakes** - Ravel is directly disobeying orders, not just being passive
2. **Active pursuit** - Creates cat-and-mouse tension throughout Act 2
3. **Dual purpose** - Ravel "hunting" Dex is cover for passing him information
4. **Plausible deniability** - If caught, Ravel can claim he was trying to recapture him

### The Key Scene Structure:
**Scene 0002_0008 - Ravel's Choice**
- Ravel has Dex in transport (ordered to dispose of him)
- Begins the Emma monologue/backstory
- Gets interrupted by kill-mode Aivia OR decides to cut Dex loose
- Either way, Dex escapes with Ravel's tacit permission

### Throughout Act 2:
- Ravel appears to be hunting Dex
- But he's actually:
  - Feeding him information
  - Keeping other agencies off his trail
  - Buying him time
  - Eventually revealing he has evidence/access Dex needs

### Character Complexity:
- Ravel isn't just broken and passive
- He's ACTIVELY defying orders while maintaining cover
- He's playing both sides - pretending to hunt while actually helping
- Creates dramatic irony when Dex doesn't initially realize Ravel is an ally

### The Emotional Core:
- Ravel couldn't act alone (compromised, watched, threatened family)
- Dex is already burned, has technical skills, has nothing left to lose
- Ravel sets him loose as a weapon he can't be himself
- Then "hunts" him as cover while actually helping

This makes Ravel a much more active, complex character rather than just a tragic backstory who helps once.
