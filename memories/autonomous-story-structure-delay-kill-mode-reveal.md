> AUTONOMOUS - Story Structure: Delay Kill Mode Reveal

AUTONOMOUS - Narrative Tension: Delay "Kill Mode" Reveal

## Critical Story Note: Preserve Mystery

### The Problem with Early "Kill Mode Aivia":
If we show an Aivia Ghost in obvious "kill mode" too early (like during Dex's escape), we reveal the central conspiracy before the audience has time to question whether the accidents are really accidents.

### What We Want the Audience to Believe (Act 1):
- Aivia crashes are tragic accidents
- Maybe software bugs, maybe coincidence
- Dex is being framed as a scapegoat
- The conspiracy is about COVERING UP accidents, not causing them

### The Reveal Should Come Later:
- Audience should discover alongside Dex that these aren't accidents
- The backdoor isn't just for surveillance - it's for assassination
- This revelation is a major turning point (probably end of Act 1 / start of Act 2)

### Dex's Escape Should Be:
- Ravel letting him go (character-driven, quiet)
- Maybe a "normal" car accident that could be coincidence
- Maybe Nova rescues him from regular FBI pursuit
- But NOT an obviously weaponized autonomous vehicle

### When to Reveal Kill Mode:
- After Dex has begun investigating
- When he discovers the backdoor code
- When he realizes the pattern isn't accidents
- THEN show an Aivia actively hunting him
- This makes the threat escalate naturally

### Scene 0002_0008 Should Be:
- Ravel's character moment (the research, the choice)
- Letting Dex go voluntarily
- Setting up the "hunt" dynamic
- NO kill mode Aivia interruption

This preserves the mystery and lets the conspiracy reveal itself gradually rather than showing the hand too early.
