> AUTONOMOUS Film: Jammer Portability - Nouveau Can Carry One

## AUTONOMOUS Film: Jammer Weight & Portability - Technical Specs

### Question: Is a signal jammer portable enough for Nouveau to carry, or must it be stationary?

**Answer: BOTH options are realistic - depends on power/range requirements**

---

### **Portable/Handheld Jammers**

**Ultra-Light Pocket Models:**
- Weight: **290 grams (0.64 lbs)** - fits in pocket
- Size: 95mm x 48mm x 17mm (smaller than a phone)
- Range: Up to 10 meters
- Battery: 2 hours
- Use case: Concealment, personal protection

**Handheld RF Jammers:**
- Weight: **3.2 lbs (1.45 kg)**
- Size: 190mm x 126mm x 46mm (7.5 x 5 x 1.8 inches)
- Can be carried like a large walkie-talkie
- More power than pocket models

---

### **Backpack Jammers (Military/Tactical)**

**Standard Military Backpack Jammer (TSJ-ManPack HP):**
- Weight: **30 kg (66 lbs)** including battery
- Dimensions: 39 x 34 x 13 cm
- Output Power: 130W total (up to 5 bands)
- Battery Life: 2 hours continuous use
- Range: Significantly larger than handheld models
- Used by: Military, law enforcement, bomb disposal units
- Wearable: Yes, designed as backpack system

**Heavy Backpack Models:**
- Weight: **25-26 kg (55-57 lbs)** typical range
- Size: 500 x 305 x 457 mm (fits in Pelican case)
- Battery: 24V/20Ah LiFePO4, 60 minutes runtime
- Professional/military grade

**Stationary High-Power Models:**
- Weight: **20+ kg (44+ lbs)**
- NOT designed for portability
- Intended for fixed deployment

---

### **Screenplay Application: Katalin Nouveau**

**Realistic Options for Nouveau:**

**Option 1: Pocket/Concealed Jammer (Most Realistic for Her Character)**
- ✅ **290g-3.2 lbs** - easily carried in jacket, bag, or holster
- ✅ Concealable - looks like power bank or phone
- ✅ Lightweight enough to run with
- ✅ 10-meter range sufficient for close encounters
- ✅ Fits ex-engineer character who'd have access to tech
- ⚠️ Limited range - needs to be close to target

**Option 2: Backpack Military Jammer (If She Needs Range)**
- ✅ **30 kg (66 lbs)** - heavy but portable
- ✅ Worn as backpack, hands-free operation
- ✅ Much greater range and power
- ✅ 2 hours battery life
- ⚠️ Obvious, not concealable
- ⚠️ Tiring to carry during action sequences
- ⚠️ Harder to explain how she acquired military-grade equipment

**Option 3: Off Grid Man Has Stationary, Nouveau Has Portable**
- ✅ **Best of both worlds**
- ✅ Off Grid Man: Fixed high-power installation protecting his property
- ✅ Nouveau: Pocket/handheld jammer for tactical use in the field
- ✅ Shows different applications of same technology
- ✅ Nouveau's is something she could realistically build/acquire

---

### **Recommended Approach for AUTONOMOUS:**

**Off Grid Man's Setup:**
- **Stationary high-power jammer** protecting his compound
- Creates a "dead zone" around his property
- Explains why Boston Dynamics dogs stop at the perimeter
- Permanently installed, powered by generator/solar

**Katalin Nouveau's Setup:**
- **Pocket-sized tactical jammer** (290g-3.2 lbs)
- Something she built or modified herself (fits engineer background)
- Concealable, can be carried during action sequences
- Limited range (10-30 meters) creates tension - she needs to get close
- Can disable individual Ghosts in kill mode when within range
- Battery limitation creates time pressure

**Dramatic Benefits:**
1. **Portability**: Nouveau can carry it during chase scenes
2. **Limitation**: Close-range requirement creates danger/tension
3. **Believability**: Lightweight tech she could realistically have
4. **Visual**: Can be palm-sized, not bulky equipment
5. **Character Consistency**: Ex-security engineer would have/build this

### **Technical Verdict:**

✅ **YES - Nouveau can absolutely carry a portable jammer**

- Pocket models: **290g-3.2 lbs** (ultra-portable)
- Backpack models: **25-30 kg** (portable but heavy)
- Both are realistic and used by military/law enforcement
